Controls

WASD move (walk straight into buildings through doors and windows, climb the stairs), Space jump, mouse aim and fire, R reload, hold right mouse to aim down sights (snipers get a full scope and a slower, longer aim), 1 main, 2 sidearm, 3 grenade, 4 medkit (click to use), 6 sledgehammer (click to swing: smashes walls and floors and hits people), 5 blocks (click to place, jump and look down to stack, right-click breaks blocks you placed), G throw a grenade, H use a medkit, B / Esc / Tab pauses. Deploy goes fullscreen and locks the mouse to the camera; Esc releases it and opens the menu, where you can Resume or Return to Menu. Dying also shows a Return to Menu button. Loadout is locked during a round and teams are random each round. First team to 25 kills wins. RPG-class weapons explode and blast holes in walls. If the mouse will not lock, hold right mouse to look.
